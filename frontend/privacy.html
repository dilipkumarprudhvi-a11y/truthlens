Privacy & Data Retention Policy

TruthLens AI is built with privacy-by-design principles. We do not store, monetize, or sell personal user data.

Article Retention: Submitted article texts are processed in volatile server memory for the duration of the analysis request. We do not permanently store raw article bodies in our primary database; only anonymous scan IDs, short 90-character snippets, and statistical aggregates are retained for audit history.

No User Tracking: TruthLens does not require user registration or deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies.

Data Deletion: Users can clear local and server audit logs anytime using the "Clear History" button on the dashboard.
